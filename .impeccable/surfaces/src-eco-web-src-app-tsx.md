# Storefront (Long Cycle)

## Scope and mode

The storefront home and the shared app shell that every later surface inherits (listing, product, bag, checkout, account, admin). Visitor mode is **Persuade** for the home first viewport and **Operate** for the shopping flows behind it. Built code-led: no image generation was available.

## Audience, job, action

- **Who:** a recruiter on a phone, between tasks, judging in minutes whether this is a real, well-made store.
- **Action:** the first viewport carries a working Add to bag for the featured basic (colour and size chosen in place).
- **Proof:** designed states (sold-out sizes, low stock, declined payment) shown where they happen. There are no invented certifications, metrics or reviews (PRODUCT.md, Evidence on Hand).

## Brand

**Long Cycle**, a fictional label of sustainable everyday basics: tops, knitwear, outerwear and trousers.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Basics built for years of washing, sold in the launderette where clothes are looked after. It refuses the category default, an oatmeal-and-serif hero photo above a four-up grid.

OWN-WORLD: White glazed tile ground on a visible grout grid. Bottle-green enamel signage with off-white condensed caps. Brushed-steel panels holding porthole frames (circular product windows). Numbered machine stickers. Archivo throughout: condensed width for signage and machine numbers, normal width for reading, with tabular figures for every readout. The palette follows a law, one colour per state: amber is low stock or in progress, red needs action, and colour is never decoration.

STORY: The visitor sees one clear clothing label (Long Cycle) and understands "well-made basics, built to last" in one line. They choose a colour and size on machine 01's panel, add to bag, and walk along the row of machines into the catalogue.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The enamel sign spans the top: wordmark, then the hook. Below it, machine 01 fills the phone screen: a large porthole holding the featured garment, then its steel control panel with a colour dial, size buttons (sold-out ones unlit but present) and a full-width Add to bag. The bag readout sits in the sign. Machines 02 and 03 begin at the fold.

FORM: The Launderette, position 3 on the ordered list; seed key d4d63287. Raises kept: ghost cells for sold-out sizes; palette law; tabular measured readouts; errors shown on the machine's own display.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Signature interaction

On Add to bag, the garment in the porthole makes one slow quarter-turn, like a drum settling, while the bag readout steps its count. With reduced motion there is no rotation and the readout simply updates.
